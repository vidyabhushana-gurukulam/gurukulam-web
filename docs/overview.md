# Vidyabhushana Gurukulam — Project Overview

**Document status:** Discovery complete (8 August 2026) · No code written yet
**Deliverable:** Public-facing informational website with an admission enquiry form
**Domain:** `vidyabhushanagurukulam.com` (purchased)

---

## 1. What This Project Is

Vidyabhushana Gurukulam is a new **day school** opening in **Vadodara, Gujarat**, with its first batch beginning in **June 2027**. It teaches the NCERT syllabus alongside a Vedic and cultural curriculum designed in-house by the founding team, based on the ISKCON gurukula model.

This project is the school's **first website**. Its job is to exist before the school does — to introduce a school that has not yet opened, establish that it is serious and credible, and collect admission enquiries from parents in Vadodara during the enquiry season that precedes the June 2027 intake.

The website is **not** a school management system. There is no fee payment, no parent login, no attendance or results. It is an informational site with one conversion action.

The founding team has run a **Sunday school in Vadodara for five years, teaching around 300 children**, with spiritual activities and character development.

## 3. The Educational Model

The defining structure of the school is how the day is divided. Sadhana comes first, academics occupy the largest block, and physical activity closes the day.

### Daily timetable

```
08:00 ── 10:00    Vedic education        (2 hrs)
10:00 ── 16:00    NCERT academics        (6 hrs)
16:00 ── 17:00    Kreeda / sports        (1 hr)
```

| Group | Timings |
|---|---|
| Pre-primary | 09:00 – 12:00 (shorter day) |
| Classes 1 – 5 | 08:00 – 17:00 (full day) |

Two things about this schedule matter for how the website is written:

1. **The day begins with sadhana, not with syllabus.** That sequencing is the school's identity in a single fact, and communicates more than any paragraph of philosophy.
2. **Six full hours go to NCERT academics** — more than many mainstream schools. This directly answers the parent's unspoken fear that a gurukula dilutes "real" studies. It should be stated plainly rather than left to be inferred.

### Teaching approach

NCERT subjects are taught **practically, not by rote memorisation** — using visual projects and 3D models to explain scientific concepts. This is a core content pillar, and it is also the most photographable thing the school does: a child holding a model they built is stronger home-page material than any statement about experiential learning.

## 8. Positioning and Tone — Binding Rules

### Strategic anchor

> **Vedic knowledge with modern science.**

Every competing gurukula implicitly asks parents to choose tradition *instead of* their child's future. This school refuses that trade-off, and saying so out loud wins the parent who is torn between the two. This is the single most important idea on the website.

### What the school is responding to

The stakeholder's motivation, in summary: today's curriculum lacks character and quality development; there is too little personal attention and no spiritual culture; children are not developed across the five koshas; there is a need for a safe environment with fewer distractions; education should be understood rather than memorised — *"not books stuffed in the brain without logic"*; devotee teachers bring character along with spirituality; the effort is framed as a mission in the spirit of Srila Prabhupada's work.

### Tone rules — these are not stylistic preferences

1. **Positive framing only.** The website must **never criticise mainstream or modern schools.** The stakeholder's critique was context for planning, not website copy. Parents reading the site have children currently enrolled in those schools; attacking their present choice makes them defensive rather than curious. Contrast is communicated by describing what this school offers, never by naming what others lack.

2. **No health claims.** Do not claim the school solves depression, anxiety, or attention disorders. Reframe as environment rather than cure: a screen-free campus, small classes, personal attention, and a settled daily rhythm. This is more credible and completely honest.

3. **Everything must be literally true.** Parents verify claims on a campus visit. Planned facilities are described as plans. Off-campus activities are described as off-campus. The Sunday school is credited to the team.

4. **Name the humans.** Not yet supplied (see Open Items), but a school website with no named people reads as anonymous. Parents want to know who is responsible for their child.

## 9. Website Strategy

| Decision | Value |
|---|---|
| **Primary goal** | Admission enquiry — every page funnels to one call to action |
| **Phase 1 scope** | Informational site + enquiry form only |
| **Explicitly excluded** | Online fee payment, parent login, attendance, exam results, homework/LMS, online donations |
| **Language** | English only |
| **Maintenance** | Maintained directly by the developer — no CMS required |
| **Requirements** | Strong SEO and search ranking, fast loading, Google Analytics |
| **Enquiry handling** | Email notification plus a Google Sheet record |

The stakeholder's instruction was explicit: *"we just want to create an initial site for now."* No roadmap or Phase 2 section is included in this document by request.

### Enquiry form and spam

The agreed approach — details deferred to the technical session — is that form submissions are handled **server-side**, never through an endpoint exposed in browser JavaScript, with a honeypot field, Cloudflare Turnstile, and rate limiting. Email gives immediate alerts; the Sheet gives a permanent record of the admission season.

### Timeline

```
Aug 2026          ← discovery complete (today)
Oct – Nov 2026    website live, search presence established
Dec 2026 – Mar 2027   admission enquiry season   ← the real deadline
Jun 2027          first batch begins
```

The operative deadline is **not** the June 2027 opening. Gujarat parents decide between roughly December and March, so the site must be live and indexed by **October–November 2026**. Search ranking takes months to build, which makes an early launch worth more than a polished late one.

## 12. Brand and Logo

The logo **design is approved**. It currently exists as an AI-generated raster image; a designer is producing a proper vector SVG.

**Design:** A circular crest in navy blue and gold on cream. A blue jewel — a literal play on *Vidya-bhushana*, the ornament of knowledge — set within a gold lotus mandala, above an open book, framed by a gold laurel wreath, with a banner reading "Vidyabhushana GURUKULAM".

**Sanskrit:** Bhagavad-gita 4.34 — *tad viddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā*. **Confirmed verified** by the stakeholder.

**Palette note:** Navy and gold is a deliberate move away from saffron conventions toward an identity that reads as a serious school rather than a religious institution. This is a strategic advantage with Vadodara parents comparing the school against mainstream private options.

**Still required from the designer:**

- Full crest, in vector
- Horizontal lockup for the site header
- Simplified jewel-only mark for the favicon
- Transparent background on all variants

**Site visual direction:** Deferred. Reference points named by the stakeholder are the Vrindavan gurukula, Mayapur gurukula, and other ISKCON gurukula websites.
