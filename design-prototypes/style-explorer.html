Why the gurukulam

An education that shapes character

Three commitments shape everything we teach and every hour of the school day.

Vedic and modern together

Children learn Sanskrit, scripture and sadhana alongside the full NCERT syllabus. Nothing is set aside to make room.

Learning by doing

Concepts are taught through models, visual projects and practical work rather than memorisation.

Teachers who are devotees

Our teachers are practising devotees and fully qualified academically. The same care runs through both halves of the day.

Thirty qualities

The qualities we cultivate

Thirty qualities of character are woven through the years a child spends here, gathered into four movements.

Qualities 1 — 9

Character & Conduct

Truthfulness, mercy, tolerance, non-violence, control of the mind and senses, and discrimination between right and wrong.

Qualities 10 — 14

Discipline & Self-Control

Celibacy, charity, reading of scripture, simplicity and satisfaction.

Qualities 15 — 21

Detachment & Discernment

Service to saintly persons, avoiding unnecessary talk, considering the self beyond the body, and sharing food with all living beings.

Qualities 22 — 30

Devotion

Hearing, chanting, remembering, serving, worship, offering obeisances and surrender — the nine processes of devotional service.

Photograph — farm visit

Once a week, the classroom is a farm

Krishi and Gau Seva are not learned from a textbook. Every week the children travel to a farm to care for cows and work the soil — the same lessons in patience, responsibility and gratitude that a page cannot teach.
